Autobiography

中文自傳

01

關於我

具備跨領域學習與工作背景，過去曾累積 6 年教育、 1 年多法律行政與約 2 年生成式 AI 應用等實務經驗。 這些經歷讓我培養出快速學習、系統化思考、 跨部門溝通與流程規劃能力。

02

學生時期（2012–2020）── 培養邏輯分析、跨部門溝通協作與團隊合作能力

求學期間，我先後接受哲學與法律訓練。 台大哲學系的訓練培養我的邏輯思考、 批判分析與問題拆解能力；政大法律系的學習， 則使我建立基礎法律思維、文字表達與嚴謹分析能力。

除了課業學習外，我也積極參與校園自治與大型活動籌辦， 希望透過不同挑戰累積溝通協調、資源整合與團隊合作能力。 曾擔任台大哲學系學會總務及松山校友會行政， 負責活動規劃、行政管理及跨單位協調。

2013 年擔任 Chameleon 四系聯合舞會總召時， 整合哲學、大氣、戲劇及生科四系資源， 在兩個月內完成活動企劃、行政流程、場地規劃及宣傳工作， 並成功爭取政府補助與企業贊助， 累積專案管理、資源整合及跨部門協作能力。

此外，我也曾赴上海交通大學參與暑期交流課程， 透過跨文化學習與國際交流拓展視野， 也提升自己適應不同環境與溝通合作的能力。

03

教育工作（2012–2020）── 從教學中建立使用者思維

求學及工作初期，我投入教育產業， 擔任英文教材編輯及英文家教老師。

除了教材設計之外，我也長期負責學生教學， 最高曾連續三個月每週七天授課。 教學過程中，我必須根據學生程度調整教材內容、 安排學習進度，並與家長保持密切溝通， 因此培養出良好的同理心、需求分析能力， 以及清楚表達複雜概念的能力。

這段經歷讓我深刻體會到， 教育並不是單純傳遞知識， 而是透過良好的內容設計與流程規劃， 幫助學習者更有效率地理解與應用知識， 也為日後投入 AI 教育產品奠定重要基礎。

04

法律事務所（2022.7–2023.10）── 延伸法律訓練，養成嚴謹的流程管理能力

承接政大法律系的學習背景， 我之後進入萬國法律事務所擔任商標程序助理， 將過去在法律訓練中培養的邏輯分析、 文件理解與嚴謹思維， 實際應用於商標案件與行政流程管理中。

工作內容包含商標申請、案件追蹤、 中英文書信往來、文件整理及行政流程控管， 並協助國內外客戶與律師之間的溝通協調。

法律案件十分重視細節、時效與文件正確性， 因此這段經歷讓我養成重視品質、 流程與時間管理的工作習慣， 也累積跨部門合作、對外溝通及案件追蹤的實務經驗。

我也因此更加理解， 完善的流程設計能有效降低錯誤、提升效率， 並讓複雜任務被清楚管理與執行。

05

AI Prompt 規劃師（2024.3–2025.11）── 從出版與教材內容延伸至 AI 應用規劃

因為對出版、教材內容與語言學習產業有興趣， 我進入希伯崙股份有限公司， 並在工作中接觸到生成式 AI 於教育產品中的應用。

這段經歷讓我從原本熟悉的內容設計與教材規劃出發， 逐步學習如何透過 Prompt Engineering、 AI Workflow 與產品流程設計， 將 AI 技術應用於語言學習、 評分回饋與教材內容生成等情境。

工作期間，我主要負責生成式 AI 在教育產品中的應用規劃與內容設計。 我設計 CEFR A1 至 A2 多階段兒童 AI 口說練習情境， 建立 AI 學習流程， 協助補習班導入生成式 AI 教學模式， 提升學生練習意願與教師教學效率。

除了語言學習產品之外， 我也負責 AI 圖像生成優化， 重新設計 Prompt 架構， 使生成內容更符合教材需求， 並成功導入 LiveABC 全國補習班教材。

相關成果同時拓展至日本市場， 包含參與日本 EIKEN 英檢教材合作， 以及韓國出版社合作專案。

此外，我參與設計 GEPT 初、中級複試 AI 評分系統， 建立寫作與口說題型的 AI 評分邏輯、 Prompt Flow 及回饋格式， 使學生能快速掌握答題缺失， 同時協助教師提升批改效率與評量一致性。

透過上述專案，我累積 Prompt Engineering、 大型語言模型（LLM）應用、需求分析、產品規劃、 跨部門溝通及 AI Workflow 設計等經驗。

我也逐漸發現， 自己對 AI 應用如何從內容規劃走向產品功能與系統實作 產生高度興趣， 因此希望未來朝 AI 應用實作與軟體開發方向發展。

06

職業訓練（2026.5–2026.8）── 補強程式開發與 AI 應用落地能力

為了從 AI 應用規劃進一步走向實際開發， 我參加勞動部勞動力發展署中彰投分署 「生成式 AI 與全端程式設計班」訓練課程。

課程內容包含 HTML、CSS、JavaScript、 Bootstrap、jQuery 等前端基礎， 也接觸 PHP、Laravel、Python、 資料庫操作與 API 串接。

透過課程，我逐步理解 Web 應用從介面規劃、 畫面設計、資料傳遞、後端處理， 到資料庫儲存的基本流程。

受訓期間，我也系統性學習生成式 AI 應用、 Python 程式設計、API 串接、前後端開發、 資料庫操作、響應式網頁製作， 以及 Figma 介面設計與原型製作基礎。

這段訓練讓我補強程式設計、 介面規劃與系統開發基礎， 也讓我更清楚理解 AI 應用如何從概念與流程設計， 進一步推進到實際功能開發。

過去的工作經驗使我理解使用者需求、 AI 應用情境與產品規劃； 職業訓練則補足我在程式開發、API 串接、 介面理解與系統實作上的基礎， 讓我能朝 AI 應用工程師與軟體工程師的方向持續前進。

07

未來發展方向

從教育、法律到 AI， 看似不同的工作內容， 其實都圍繞著同一件事── 運用知識、流程與技術解決問題。

在 AI Prompt 規劃工作的過程中， 我逐漸發現自己不只希望參與 AI 產品設計， 也希望更深入理解系統如何被開發， 並親自參與 AI 應用的實作。

因此，我透過職業訓練補強 Python、 API 串接、前後端開發、資料庫操作， 以及 Figma 介面設計基礎， 期望從 AI 應用規劃逐步轉向軟體開發職涯。

未來，我希望投入生成式 AI 應用工程師、 AI 應用開發工程師、初階後端工程師 或 Web 應用工程師等職位， 持續深化 Python、API 串接、Laravel、 資料庫、LLM 應用、AI Workflow 設計 與 Web 介面實作能力。

我的目標是成為一位能理解使用者需求、 規劃 AI 應用流程，並具備實作能力的軟體工程師， 協助團隊開發真正能落地並解決問題的 AI 產品。

Education Experience (2012–2020) — Developing a User-Centered Mindset through Teaching

During my studies and the early stage of my career, I worked in the education industry as an English educational materials editor and private English tutor.

These responsibilities helped me develop empathy, requirement-analysis skills, and the ability to explain complex concepts in a clear and understandable way.

This experience taught me that education is not simply about delivering knowledge. Effective content design and workflow planning are essential for helping learners understand and apply knowledge efficiently.

This user-centered perspective later became an important foundation for my work on AI-powered educational products.

Law Firm Experience (July 2022–October 2023) — Building Rigorous Workflow Management Skills

Building on my legal education at National Chengchi University, I joined Formosa Transnational Attorneys at Law as a trademark procedure assistant.

In this role, I applied the logical analysis, document comprehension, and attention to detail developed through my legal training to trademark cases and administrative workflow management.

My responsibilities included trademark applications, case tracking, Chinese and English correspondence, document organization, and administrative process control.

I also assisted with communication and coordination between domestic and international clients and attorneys.

Legal cases require a high level of accuracy, timeliness, and document quality. This experience helped me develop disciplined habits in quality management, workflow control, and time management.

It also gave me practical experience in cross-departmental collaboration, external communication, and case tracking.

Through this role, I gained a deeper understanding of how well-designed workflows can reduce errors, improve efficiency, and make complex tasks easier to manage and execute.

05

AI Prompt Strategist (March 2024–November 2025) — Extending Educational Content Experience into AI Application Planning

Because of my interest in publishing, educational content, and language-learning products, I joined Hebron Soft Limited, where I began working with generative AI applications in educational products.

Building on my previous experience in content design and educational material planning, I gradually learned how to apply Prompt Engineering, AI workflows, and product process design to language learning, automated assessment, feedback generation, and educational content creation.

My primary responsibilities included planning generative AI applications and designing content for educational products.

I designed multi-stage AI speaking practice scenarios for children at CEFR A1 to A2 levels and established structured AI-assisted learning workflows.

I also supported the implementation of generative AI teaching models in language schools, helping increase students' willingness to practice and improve teachers' instructional efficiency.

In addition to language-learning products, I was responsible for optimizing AI image generation. I redesigned prompt structures to produce visual content that better matched educational material requirements.

The optimized image-generation process was successfully introduced into LiveABC educational materials used by language schools across Taiwan.

The work was later expanded to international projects, including collaboration on Japanese EIKEN preparation materials and projects with a Korean publishing company.

I also participated in the design of an AI assessment system for the intermediate and high-intermediate stages of the General English Proficiency Test (GEPT).

I helped establish AI scoring logic, Prompt Flows, and feedback formats for writing and speaking tasks. The system allowed students to identify weaknesses more quickly while helping teachers improve grading efficiency and assessment consistency.

Through these projects, I gained practical experience in Prompt Engineering, large language model applications, requirement analysis, product planning, cross-departmental communication, and AI workflow design.

I also became increasingly interested in how AI applications could move beyond content planning and become functional software products.

This realization motivated me to pursue a future career in AI application development and software engineering.

Vocational Training (May 2026–August 2026) — Strengthening Software Development and AI Implementation Skills

To move from AI application planning toward practical software development, I enrolled in the Generative AI and Full-Stack Programming Training Program provided by the Taichung-Changhua-Nantou Regional Branch of Taiwan's Workforce Development Agency.

Through the training, I gradually developed an understanding of the complete workflow of a Web application, from interface planning and visual design to data transmission, back-end processing, and database storage.

I also systematically studied generative AI applications, Python programming, API integration, front-end and back-end development, database operations, responsive Web design, and the fundamentals of interface design and prototyping with Figma.

This training strengthened my foundation in programming, interface planning, and system development.

It also gave me a clearer understanding of how an AI application can progress from an initial concept and workflow design to the implementation of functional software features.

My previous professional experience helped me understand user requirements, AI application scenarios, and product planning.

The vocational training complemented this background by strengthening my skills in software development, API integration, interface implementation, and system development.

Together, these experiences have prepared me to continue developing toward a career as an AI application engineer and software engineer.

07

Future Career Direction

Education, law, and AI may appear to be unrelated fields, but my work in each of them has centered on the same goal: using knowledge, workflows, and technology to solve practical problems.

While working as an AI Prompt Strategist, I realized that I did not only want to participate in AI product planning. I also wanted to understand how systems are developed and contribute directly to the implementation of AI applications.

I therefore strengthened my foundation in Python, API integration, front-end and back-end development, database operations, and Figma interface design through vocational training.

My goal is to gradually transition from AI application planning into a software development career.

In the future, I hope to pursue opportunities as a generative AI application engineer, AI application developer, junior back-end engineer, or Web application engineer.

I plan to continue developing my skills in Python, API integration, Laravel, databases, large language model applications, AI workflow design, and Web interface implementation.

My long-term goal is to become a software engineer who can understand user requirements, plan effective AI application workflows, and implement practical software solutions.

I hope to contribute to teams by developing AI products that can be deployed in real-world environments and solve meaningful problems.
